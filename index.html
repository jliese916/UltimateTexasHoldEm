Flop Strategies

El Jefe Strategy Intermediate

  1. Raise 2x with a straight or better.
  2. Raise with a hidden pair made with a non-pocket starting hand.
  3. With 22, raise when the flop contains a deuce or the flop itself is three of a kind; otherwise check.
  4. Raise with four to a flush containing a hidden ten or higher, except a bare ten-high flush draw with an offsuit Tx hand, no pair, and no one-card straight draw.
  5. With T9 or J9, raise an eight-out straight draw unless both hole cards are higher than every flop card.
  6. With 96s, 97s, or 98s, raise any four-flush.
  7. With K2o, K3o, or K4o, raise K-high on an ace-containing paired flop or a three-of-a-kind flop.
  8. Otherwise check.

Wizard of Odds Strategy Beginner

  1. Raise 2x with two pair or better.
  2. Raise with a hidden pair, except pocket deuces.
  3. Raise with four to a flush containing a hidden ten or higher.
  4. Otherwise check.
