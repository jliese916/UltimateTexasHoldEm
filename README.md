# Casa del Jefe — The Ultimate Hold’em Parlor v13

A self-contained Ultimate Texas Hold’em app with Play, Train, Look Up, and a 100-hand El Jefe Challenge.

## Version 12 changes

- Train starts with the same “Press Deal to begin.” text as Play.
- The Train table masthead is styled as TRAINING ROOM to match the other Casa del Jefe trainers.
- The El Jefe Challenge is enabled for 100 complete decision hands.
- Scores of 95% or better earn certification; 100% earns the Grand Master certificate.
- Look Up begins directly with instructions and no Current Decision badge.
- Strategy references are organized into three blackjack-style collapsible guides: Optimal Preflop Play, Flop Strategies, and River Strategies.
- The preflop chart uses descending rows, ascending columns, one triangular half, pairs on the diagonal, and 4XS notation.

- Tightened the preflop chart so it fits desktop screens without horizontal scrolling.
- Preflop actions are fixed as Check on the left and Raise 4x on the right; later streets keep the same left/right decision positions.


## v13 changes
- Challenge mode now records the first mistake silently but lets the player finish the hand naturally.
- Decisions after the first mistake do not affect grading.
- The Training Room wordmark has been reduced to match the other Casa del Jefe trainers.
