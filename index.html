Flop Strategies

Flop Strategy Terms

Hidden pair
A pair formed by matching at least one of your hole cards with a card on the flop.
Four-card flush
Four of the five known cards—your two hole cards and the three flop cards—have the same suit.
Outside straight draw
Four consecutive ranks that can complete a straight at either end. This is also called an open-ended straight draw.
Inside straight draw
A straight draw that needs a missing rank from inside the sequence.
Double-inside straight draw
Two different inside ranks can complete a straight.

El Jefe Strategy Intermediate

Apply these rules in order. Raise 2x when the first applicable rule tells you to raise.

  1. Straight or better: Raise.
  2. Pocket deuces: With 22, raise if the flop contains a deuce or if the flop is three of a kind. Otherwise, check.
  3. Hidden pair: Raise.
  4. Strong four-card flush: Raise when you have four cards to a flush and at least one of your hole cards in that suit is a ten or higher.
    Exception: With an offsuit Tx hand, check when the ten is your only hole card in the flush suit, you have no pair, and you have no inside or outside straight draw.
  5. T9 or J9 straight draws: With T9 or J9, raise with an outside or double-inside straight draw.
    Exception: Check when both hole cards are higher than every card on the flop.
  6. Small suited hands: With 96s, 97s, or 98s, raise with any four-card flush.
  7. Small offsuit kings on an ace-containing paired flop: With K2o, K3o, or K4o, raise when the flop contains both an ace and a pair. This includes a pair of aces.
  8. Small offsuit kings on a three-of-a-kind flop: With K2o, K3o, or K4o, raise when all three flop cards have the same rank.
  9. Everything else: Check.

Wizard of Odds Strategy Beginner

  1. Raise 2x with two pair or better.
  2. Raise with a hidden pair, except pocket deuces.
  3. Raise with a four-card flush when at least one of your hole cards in that suit is a ten or higher.
  4. Otherwise check.
River Strategies

River Strategy Terms

One-card outs
The number of unseen individual cards that would give the dealer a stronger hand if that card were the dealer’s only hole card.
A hole card contributes
Removing that hole card weakens your final best five-card hand, including its kicker.
Higher and lower hole cards
Determined by rank.
Dry board
No three board cards share a suit.
Four-flush board
At least four board cards share a suit.
Hidden pair of the higher card
You have exactly one pair because your higher hole-card rank appears on the board.
Hidden pair of the lower card
You have exactly one pair because your lower hole-card rank appears on the board.

El Jefe Strategy Intermediate

Count the one-card outs, then apply the first matching rule.

Global Rules

  • 18 or fewer outs: Call.
  • Board quads: Call through 28 outs when a hole card contributes your kicker.
  • Pocket deuces making two pair: Call through 23 outs when the deuces contribute to your final hand.

Exactly 19 Outs

First, fold if neither hole card contributes. Otherwise:

  • Pocket deuces: Call when a deuce contributes.
  • 9-high, T-high, or J-high starting hand: Call.
  • Q-high starting hand: Call, except fold when your final hand is only high card, only the queen contributes, and exactly three board cards share a suit.
  • K-high starting hand: Call only when your final hand is exactly one pair and only the king contributes.

Otherwise, fold.

Exactly 20 Outs

Starting hand is 8-high or stronger

Call when at least one hole card contributes, except for the following trap.

Weak Lower-Pair Trap — Fold when all five are true:
  • Your starting hand is 8-high, 9-high, or T-high.
  • Your final hand is exactly one pair.
  • Your lower hole-card rank makes that pair.
  • Both hole cards contribute, with the higher card playing as a kicker.
  • Fewer than four board cards share a suit.
Starting hand is 7-high or weaker

Call in any of these situations:

  • Your final hand is two pair and a hole card contributes.
  • Your higher hole card makes the pair, and either the higher card is a six or seven or the board is dry.
  • Your lower hole card makes the pair, only that lower card contributes, and the board is dry.

Otherwise, fold.

Exactly 21 Outs

Call in either situation:

  • Your final hand is exactly one pair, your higher hole card makes that pair, and the higher card is an eight or better.
  • Your starting hand is 86 or 87; 95, 96, 97, or 98; T3 or higher; J3 or higher; Q3 or higher; or K3 or higher among the preflop-check hands.

Suitedness does not change these starting-hand groups. Otherwise, fold.

More Than 21 Outs

Fold unless one of the global exceptions applies: pocket deuces making two pair through 23 outs, or board quads with a contributing hole-card kicker through 28 outs.

Wizard of Odds Strategy Beginner

  1. Call 1x with a hidden pair or better.
  2. Otherwise call when fewer than 21 one-card dealer outs beat you.
  3. Otherwise fold.
