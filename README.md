# Casa del Jefe — The Ultimate Hold’em Parlor v11

A self-contained Ultimate Texas Hold’em app with Play, Train, Look Up, and a 100-hand El Jefe Challenge.

## Version 11 changes

- Train starts with the same “Press Deal to begin.” text as Play.
- The Train table masthead is styled as an uppercase TRAIN room title.
- The El Jefe Challenge is enabled for 100 complete decision hands.
- Scores of 95% or better earn certification; 100% earns the Grand Master certificate.
- Look Up begins directly with instructions and no Current Decision badge.
- Strategy references are organized into three collapsible guides: Optimal Preflop Play, Post-Flop Play, and River Play.
- The preflop chart uses descending rows, ascending columns, one triangular half, pairs on the diagonal, and 4XS notation.
